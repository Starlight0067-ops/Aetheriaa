# AETHERIA — Narrative Bible (v0.1)

This is the canonical lore database. Every other document defers to it. Each entry carries an ID (`L-###`) so continuity can be tracked.

## 1. Cosmology

* **L-001 Aether.** A living current that runs beneath the world, like groundwater. Every living thing draws a thin thread from it.
* **L-002 The six Resonances.** The Aether "sings" in six tones, and those tones are the elements: Pyra (fire), Aqua (water), Terra (earth), Volt (lightning), Zephyr (wind) and Crya (ice). People who can hear a tone are called *Resonants*.
* **L-003 Aetherial Cores.** Seven colossal crystalline spires. Officially they "kept the balance". In truth (L-090) they were *regulators* built by the First Choir. They throttled the Aether's song so mortals could survive it.

## 2. Chronology

| Era | Years | Events |
|---|---|---|
| Age of the Unsung | ? | The Aether is wild. Storms of raw element make the surface nearly uninhabitable. |
| Age of the Choir | −1200 to −0 | The **First Choir**, a fellowship of the earliest Resonants, raises the seven Cores. Civilization blooms in their shelter. |
| **The Great Silence** | Year 0 | In a single night all seven Cores go dark. Resonance weakens worldwide. The Choir vanishes from history. |

| Present day | 612 AS | The Elyndra Core flickers for the first time in six centuries. The same night, the protagonist wakes in the Whisperwood. |

## 3. Geography

* **Elyndra, the Green Frontier.** Temperate forests, mountain spurs, a river valley, and Choir ruins. Its capital is **Vael Harrow**. (Playable in v1.)
* **Auralis, the Azure Coast.** Cliffs, harbors and archipelagos. A maritime merchant republic. (Future.)
* **Nivareth, the Frozen Crown.** Glacial highlands and fortress-monasteries. (Future.)
* **Solmara, the Sunken Horizon.** Dunes over buried Choir cities. (Future.)

### Elyndra landmarks
* **Vael Harrow.** A river town on two banks joined by the *Old Span* bridge. Its plaza has a dry Choir fountain.
* **Whisperwood.** An old forest south of town, where the protagonist wakes.
* **Silverfall.** A waterfall that feeds **Lake Mirelle**.
* **The Choir Steps.** Ruins on the eastern ridge, guarding the sealed **Hollow Sanctum** dungeon.
* **The Elyndra Core.** A dormant spire visible from almost everywhere, used as the region's navigation landmark.

## 4. Civilizations and factions

* **The Harrow Concord.** A council of guilds that governs Vael Harrow. Practical and wary of Resonants.
* **The Church of the Quiet Bell.** Teaches that the Silence was a divine mercy, and that the gods withdrew the Aether to keep mortals from destroying themselves. They see Resonance as temptation.
* **The Lanternwrights.** Scholar-engineers who study Choir devices and want to relight the Cores. Idealistic, and sometimes reckless.
* **The Hushed.** (Antagonists.) A secret order descended from the Choir members who *chose* the Silence. They keep the Cores dark because they know what the Cores really cost (L-091).

## 5. Religion and philosophy

* Quiet Bell doctrine: "What is silent cannot be broken."
* Lanternwright creed: "Every lock was made by a hand. Every hand can be understood."
* Folk belief: Silverfall's spray carries the voices of the lost, so travelers whisper names into it.

## 6. Legends

* **The Seventh Verse.** A children's song with seven verses, and nobody remembers the seventh. (The missing verse is the key to the Cores.)
* **The Lantern Maiden.** A Choir singer who walked into the Elyndra Core and "became its light". (A real event: she is the Core's anchor. See L-092.)

## 7. Characters

### Protagonist: **Kael Arden** (default name; customizable later)
* Age appears 19. Ash-silver hair, amber eyes, a teal travel coat with copper clasps, and a short single-edged blade.
* Wakes with no memory, holding a cracked tuning-crystal pendant.
* Hears **all six** Resonances faintly, which should be impossible.
* Secret (L-093): Kael was *sung into being* by the Elyndra Core as it began to wake. Kael is the Core's voice given a body, and that ties Kael's fate to every choice about relighting.

### Main characters
* **Lyra Vensel.** A Lanternwright apprentice, 22. Curious and talkative, with a habit of sketching everything. Lyra serves as the guide character. Resonance: Zephyr.
* **Elder Maren Holt.** Speaker of the Harrow Concord. Stern but fair. Lost a child to a Resonance accident.
* **Brother Ostin.** A Quiet Bell priest, gentle and sincere, who believes Kael is a warning sign.
* **Toma.** A street kid who runs messages and knows every alley.

### Antagonists
* **The Hollow Warden.** (Elyndra boss.) A Choir guardian construct of stone and dead crystal. It defends the Sanctum and has no malice, only an old order to keep everyone out.
* **Veyra of the Hushed.** A calm, persuasive Hushed envoy who is always one step ahead. Recurring antagonist.

## 8. Main story: Act I "First Verse" (Elyndra)
1. **Awakening.** Kael wakes in the Whisperwood, follows the Core's glow, and meets Lyra.
2. **Vael Harrow.** Kael meets Elder Maren and earns trust by helping the town (side tasks).
3. **The Choir Steps.** Kael and Lyra find a resonance lock that only Kael can sing open.
4. **Hollow Sanctum.** A dungeon with element puzzles. Kael defeats the Hollow Warden.
5. **The Flicker.** Kael touches the Core shard. Elyndra's Core brightens. Veyra appears and warns: "Every light you kindle, something else must pay for." (Act I ends.)

## 9. Hidden truths (reveal order)
* **L-090** (Act II): The Cores were regulators, not gifts.
* **L-091** (Act III): Each Core needs a living anchor, a Resonant who sleeps inside it forever.
* **L-092** (Act III): The Lantern Maiden is still alive inside the Elyndra Core.
* **L-093** (Finale): Kael is a Core's voice in human form.

## 10. Dialogue guidelines
* Plain, warm, slightly lyrical speech. No modern slang.
* Each character has a verbal tic: Lyra trails into tangents, Maren is clipped, Ostin quotes scripture, Toma bargains.
* Lines stay at two sentences or fewer per box. Never dump exposition. Reveal lore through objects and environment.

## 11. Continuity rules
* Year is 612 AS (After Silence).
* Nobody alive has seen an active Core.
* Resonants exist but are rare (about 1 in 400 people) and can hear only one element. Kael is the only exception.
* Changes to this file must update the IDs and the changelog below.
