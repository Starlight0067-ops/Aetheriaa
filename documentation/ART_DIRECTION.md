**Keywords:** sunlit storybook, painterly flat shading, readable silhouettes, warm-cool contrast.

## Rendering style
* Stylized, mostly flat albedo with gentle diffuse wrap. Low roughness on water and crystal.
* Strong warm sun with soft cool ambient. Light blue distance fog for depth.
* Glow (bloom) only on Aether and elemental effects.
* Characters: large heads (about 1:6 proportion), bold hair shapes, clean color blocking.

## Shape language
* **Town:** rounded, friendly, leaning roofs, chunky timber.
* **Choir ruins:** tall, thin, precise geometry, hexagonal motifs, glowing inlay.
* **Enemies:** thorny, asymmetrical, organic. Bosses read as architecture come to life.
